> **My Approach to Tools**: Don't use AI just to be trendy. Good tools should be like a hallway light switch: you tap it in the dark, it fixes the problem, and you move on with your life.  
> **Where to Put Them**: Apple Shortcuts / Android Home Screen Widgets / Browser Bookmarks

---

## Stop Downloading Ten Different AI Apps

If you open the App Store on any given day, your screen is flooded with bizarre, overhyped tools: AI horoscopes, AI tarot readings, or generic chatbots charging a \$30 monthly subscription for basic text.

The reality? You download a bunch of them, register an email for each, and two weeks later they are gathering digital dust on page four of your home screen.

What real people actually need is not another heavy, bloated app. We just need the practical vision and summarization ability of AI, trimmed down into three physical buttons on our phone screen.

After six months of trial and error in my daily life, these are the only three shortcuts that survived on my home screen.

---

## Shortcut 1: The Grocery Ingredient Decoder

* **When to use it**: Standing in the supermarket aisle holding a tub of yogurt, instant oatmeal, or a snack box. The ingredient list is printed in tiny 6-point font with thirty unpronounceable chemical names.
* **The physical action**: Tap the icon -> Point camera at the back label -> Get an honest answer in three seconds.

```text
[Attach photo of food nutrition facts & ingredients label]

Please take a look at this ingredient list and tell me in plain, honest words:
1. What are the main ingredients? Are there cheap fillers (like hydrogenated oils, palm fat, high-fructose corn syrup)?
2. The additive situation: Which ones are harmless preservatives, and which ones should I be cautious about (trans fats, hidden sodium, artificial sweeteners)?
3. One-sentence bottom line: Is this a healthy pick for normal adults, kids, or clean eating? Buy it or put it back on the shelf?
```

## Shortcut 2: The Late-Night Impulse Filter

* **When to use it**: Late at night or over the weekend, an infuriating, passive-aggressive work email lands in your inbox, or a relative sends a stinging comment in the family group. Your blood pressure spikes. The first response you type out is loaded with anger—and sending it will only start a messy fire.
* **The physical action**: Copy the annoying message -> Tap the shortcut -> Get three calm, professional draft options.

```text
The message I received:
"[Paste the annoying message here]"

I am upset right now and my instinct is to snap back defensively, but I want to stay calm and keep my dignity intact.
Please give me 3 draft options with different tones:

Option A [Polite and Cold]: Very brief, politely noting that I am offline and will follow up during normal business hours;
Option B [Stick to the Facts]: Ignores the emotional bait, focuses purely on objective details and the next step;
Option C [Firm Boundary]: Courteous, but draws a clear line so they cannot keep walking over me.
```

## Shortcut 3: The Receipt and Warranty Keeper

* **When to use it**: You just unboxed a new toaster or household appliance. The box is stuffed with tiny receipts, warranty cards, and manuals. Throw them away, and you won't be able to get repairs later; keep them, and your kitchen drawers turn into a junk drawer.
* **The physical action**: Snap a photo of the receipt and the serial number on the back of the device -> Run the shortcut -> Get a clean text block for your notes.

```text
[Attach photo of receipt and device serial number sticker]

Please extract the key details from this photo into a clean format so I can paste it into my phone notes:

- Item & Brand:
- Purchase Date:
- Warranty End Date (default to 1 year if unstated):
- Official Support Website / Phone Number:
- Receipt / Serial Number (SN):
- One Maintenance Reminder: What is the most common mistake that ruins this item?
```

## How to Put These on Your Phone (Zero Code Required)

* **On iPhone / iPad**:
  1. Open the built-in **Shortcuts** app;
  2. Tap the `+` icon in the top right -> Add action "Ask ChatGPT" or "Ask Gemini";
  3. Paste the prompt above into the instructions box;
  4. Tap the share icon at the bottom -> Select **Add to Home Screen**. That’s it!
* **On Android**:
  Open your favorite AI app in your mobile browser, save the conversation with the prompt pinned, and tap "Add to Home screen" from your browser settings.
