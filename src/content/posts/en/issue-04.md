## [The Friction]

Open the App Store on any given day, and you are inundated with bizarre, over-marketed "AI miracles": AI astrology subscriptions, AI essay generators for \$29.99/month, or generic wrappers for basic chatbots.

The reality? You download 10 separate apps, sign up for 10 accounts, and within two weeks, they gather digital dust on your phone's fourth home screen page.

**What real humans need is not another bloated app. We need the raw multimodal vision and reasoning power of frontier models, tailored into three physical trigger buttons on our phone home screens.**

After six months of rigorous personal testing, only three shortcuts earned permanent placement on my home screen.

---

## [Shortcut #1: The Grocery Ingredient Decoder]

* **Context**: Standing in the supermarket cereal or yogurt aisle. The ingredient list is printed in microscopic 6pt font with 25 unpronounceable chemical additives.
* **Physical Trigger**: Long-press home screen icon -> Snap a quick photo of the back of the box -> 3-second plain-English verdict.

```text
[Attach photo of food nutrition facts & ingredients label]

Role: Pragmatic Food Safety & Nutrition Advisor
Analyze this label with zero marketing fluff. Answer these 3 points in simple terms:

1. Baseline Quality: What are the primary ingredients? Are they cheap fillers (palm oil, high-fructose corn syrup, artificial thickeners)?
2. Additive Triage:
   - 🟢 Safe, natural ingredients
   - 🟡 Common preservatives/stabilizers (acceptable in moderation)
   - 🔴 Red flags to avoid (trans fats, stealth sugars, artificial sweeteners)
3. The Verdict: Is this suitable for [clean eating / kids / elders]? One sentence: Buy it or put it back on the shelf?
```

## [Shortcut #2: The Late-Night Emotional De-escalator]

* **Context**: At 10:30 PM on a Friday, an infuriating passive-aggressive email arrives from a client or colleague. Your adrenaline spikes. The first response you draft is dripping with rage—sending it will ignite a needless firestorm.
* **Physical Trigger**: Copy the message -> Tap the shortcut -> Receive 3 calibrated de-escalation drafts.

```text
The message I received:
"[Paste the infuriating original message]"

I am angry and tempted to lash out defensively, but I must remain professional and protect my boundaries.
Generate 3 distinct, emotionally filtered reply options:

Option A [The Minimalist Icebox]: Ultra-short, polite, neutral, deferring discussion to standard business hours;
Option B [The Fact Focus]: Ignores emotional bait; states objective facts and defines the immediate next operational step;
Option C [The Firm Boundary]: Courteously declines unreasonable scope without leaving room for debate.
```

## [Shortcut #3: The Warranty & Receipt Keeper]

* **Context**: You unbox a new appliance (vacuum, coffee maker, monitor). You are left with a receipt, warranty card, and serial number sticker. Toss them, and you risk losing warranty coverage; keep them, and your drawer becomes a graveyard of crinkled paper.
* **Physical Trigger**: Photograph the receipt and the device serial number sticker -> Run shortcut -> Automatically generates a calendar event.

```text
[Attach photo of receipt and device serial number sticker]

Task: Domestic Asset & Warranty Management
Extract the following structured details so I can archive them into Apple Notes or Google Calendar:

- Item Name & Brand:
- Purchase Date:
- Suggested Warranty Expiry Date (default 1 year unless noted):
- Official Support Contact / URL:
- Proof of Purchase Summary (Invoice # or Device SN):
- One Golden Maintenance Tip: What is the single most common mistake that breaks this item?
```

## [How to Install These Shortcuts Without Coding]

* **For iOS (iPhone/iPad)**:
  1. Open the built-in **Shortcuts** app;
  2. Tap `+` -> Add action "Ask ChatGPT" or "Ask Gemini";
  3. Paste the respective prompt into the system prompt field;
  4. Tap the share arrow -> Select **Add to Home Screen**. Done!
* **For Android**:
  Open your favorite AI app (ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude), create a dedicated chat with the prompt pinned, and choose "Add to Home Screen" from your browser or app menu.
